Privacy at Folingoo

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This policy covers the Folingoo app for Android and iOS, an English practice companion with an optional AI coach, and this website. It explains what we collect, why, who processes it and the choices you have. Contact: dandigital1688@gmail.com.

What the app collects

  • Account: your name, email address and profile photo from Google or Apple sign-in, and a user ID. Used to sign you in and show your profile.
  • Learning settings: app language, level, goal, daily minutes, English variety and reminder time. Used to build your plan and lessons.
  • Your answers to the coach: written text, and transcripts of what you say. Used to give feedback and track progress.
  • Voice recordings, only after you agree in the app. They are turned into text for feedback and deleted as soon as they are transcribed.
  • Learning history: lessons, scores, mistakes, review cards, streaks and the coach's suggestions. Used to adapt lessons and run spaced review.
  • Practice results from word games, the daily workout, reading and listening passages and missions: skill scores (for example "vocabulary recall 72"), the dates you practised, and totals such as words known and XP. Used so the coach can plan with practice done on the phone.
  • Daily usage counts, used to apply fair daily limits on AI features.

Unless noted above, this data is kept until you delete your account. The app does not collect your location, contacts or advertising ID. It has no ads, analytics or tracking SDKs, and we do not sell your data or share it for advertising.

Who processes it

We do not share your data with third parties for their own purposes. These service providers process it on our behalf, only to run the app:

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in and file storage.
  • Groq runs the AI models that write lessons and feedback and transcribe speech. Your answers, recordings and learning settings are sent to Groq only to produce that output.
  • Google and Apple handle sign-in when you choose them.

We may disclose data if the law requires it.

Stored only on your phone

  • An offline copy of your lessons and review cards, and answers waiting to be sent.
  • Reminder and notification settings. Notifications are scheduled on the device.
  • Data for the home-screen widgets: streak, today's lesson and a few review words.
  • Your on-device learning record: word progress, game results, today's workout, mission steps, XP, badges, the vocabulary check result and your voice choice. Only the skill scores and totals listed above are sent to us; the individual answers you give in games and passages stay on the phone.

Signing out clears the widgets. Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on the phone.

Your choices

  • Get a copy: Profile → "Copy my learning data" copies your on-device learning record as text. For a copy of the data in your account, email us.
  • Delete data on the phone: Profile → "Delete learning data on this phone".
  • Delete your account: Profile → Delete account permanently removes your profile, lessons, feedback, recordings, review cards and practice results from our servers. You can also ask us by email.
  • Your voice: before your first recording, the app asks whether it may send recordings for speech-to-text. Nothing is uploaded unless you agree, and you can turn it off in Profile at any time. Writing, word games and review never send audio.
  • Microphone: used only while you record an answer. You can type instead.
  • Notifications: turn them off in Profile or in your phone settings.

Children

Folingoo is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us data, email us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Database rules let each account read only its own data. Feedback written by AI is marked as an estimate; scores from games and mission steps are checked against fixed answer keys on the device.

Beta signup on this website

When you join the beta, we store your email address, the device type you choose (iOS or Android), and the time you signed up. If you sign up again with the same email, we update your device choice.

We use this information to send you beta access instructions by email. Emails are sent manually; this website does not send email automatically.

Signup details are stored in a MongoDB database and can be viewed by the Folingoo team through a password-protected admin page. To have your signup deleted, email dandigital1688@gmail.com.

The sample lesson on the home page is fixed and runs in your browser. It does not collect your input or connect to the coach.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and tell you in the app if the change matters.
