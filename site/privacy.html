Privacy

Privacy at Folingoo

Beta signup on this website

When you join the beta, we store your email address, the device type you choose (iOS or Android), and the time you signed up. If you sign up again with the same email, we update your device choice.

We use this information to send you beta access instructions by email. Emails are sent manually; this website does not send email automatically.

Signup details are stored in a MongoDB database and can be viewed by the Folingoo team through a password-protected admin page.

In the Folingoo app

Voice recordings are used to create a transcript and are deleted after transcription. Transcripts and learning progress are used by the app to give feedback, adapt future lessons, and bring items back for review.

The sample lesson on this page

The sample lesson on the home page is fixed and runs in your browser. It does not collect your input or connect to the coach.
